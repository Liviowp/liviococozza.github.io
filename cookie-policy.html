Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on liviococozza.com.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies, including pixels and local storage, may also store or access information on your device.

2. What has been identified so far?

A review of the homepage source code found no Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or other deliberate advertising/analytics tag. The homepage loads local website scripts and stylesheets and requests Google Fonts from Google's servers. A source-code review alone cannot establish every cookie or storage item set at runtime by hosting, security or third-party infrastructure.

No non-essential analytics or advertising cookies are intentionally configured by the website owner in the homepage code reviewed. This statement is subject to confirmation through a live browser cookie and storage scan, including all site pages and mobile/desktop views.

3. Consent

Strictly necessary technologies may be used where they are essential to deliver a service expressly requested or to transmit a communication. Other cookies or similar technologies that require consent must not be placed or read until you have given valid prior consent. Continuing to browse is not consent.

4. Third-party services

The website currently requests Google Fonts from Google-hosted servers. The contact form uses Web3Forms when you submit an enquiry. External links to social networks and video platforms open their respective websites. These services may process technical information under their own privacy and cookie policies. The exact cookies, if any, set by these providers in the context of this website have not yet been confirmed by a live scan.

5. Managing cookies

You can review and delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect features on websites. If this website later introduces non-essential cookies or tracking tools, this policy will be updated and a consent mechanism will be implemented before those technologies are activated.

6. Contact

For questions about this policy, contact doriamondo@gmail.com.
