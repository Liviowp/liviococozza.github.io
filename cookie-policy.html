Cookie Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This page explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on liviococozza.com.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies, including pixels and local storage, may also store or access information on your device.

2. What has been identified so far?

A Cookiebot compliance scan dated 24 November 2026 reported a Low risk status and detected zero trackers across 10 scanned pages. Its tracker breakdown showed zero items in the Necessary, Preferences, Statistics, Marketing and Unclassified categories. This is a useful automated scan result, but it is a snapshot and does not prove that no cookie or browser-storage item can ever be set in every browser, session or future version of the website.

A separate review of the website code found no deliberate Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel, Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity implementation in the pages and scripts reviewed. The website's font files are self-hosted and are not requested from Google Fonts. Flag images are hosted locally by the website.

3. Consent

Strictly necessary technologies may be used where they are essential to deliver a service expressly requested or to transmit a communication. Other cookies or similar technologies that require consent must not be placed or read until you have given valid prior consent. Continuing to browse is not consent.

4. Third-party services

The website's fonts and flag images are hosted locally. The contact form sends the information you enter to Web3Forms when you submit an enquiry; Web3Forms may also process technical information such as the submitter's IP address for form delivery and spam prevention. External links to social networks and video platforms open their respective websites; no embedded social-media feed or video player was identified in the reviewed homepage source. These providers may process technical information under their own privacy and cookie policies. The Cookiebot scan reported zero trackers on the 10 pages it scanned. The Web3Forms account settings reviewed on 24 November 2026 also showed no optional integrations enabled. These are useful snapshots, but they do not replace checks of browser storage and network behaviour in every browser or live session.

5. Managing cookies

You can review and delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking all cookies may affect features on websites. If a live technical scan confirms that this website uses only strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies, a consent banner may not be required. If any non-essential cookies or similar technologies are used, valid consent must be obtained before they are placed or read.
