Cookie Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This page explains the use of cookies and similar technologies on liviococozza.com.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device. Similar technologies, including pixels and local storage, may also store or access information on your device.

2. Cookies on this website

Based on the website's current implementation, the site itself does not set cookies and does not use local storage or similar technologies to store information on your device. It does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and images used on the site are hosted on the website itself and are not requested from third-party services.

3. Consent

Because the website does not use cookies or similar technologies, no cookie banner is shown. If this changes in the future, cookies or similar technologies that require consent will not be placed or read until you have given valid prior consent, and this page will be updated.

4. Third-party services

The contact form transmits information to Web3Forms only when you submit it. Web3Forms may also process technical information such as the submitter's IP address for form delivery and spam prevention. This form submission is described in the Privacy Policy and does not mean the site itself uses tracking cookies. External links to LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube open the respective websites; those services may use their own cookies or similar technologies after you visit them. No social-media feed or video player is embedded on this website.

5. Managing cookies

You can review and delete cookies through your browser settings. When you follow an external social-media or video link, cookie controls and privacy choices are managed on that provider's website. Blocking cookies may affect features on websites.
