Where Have All the Talents Gone?

The Decline of Youth Athletic Development

In the past, both schools and sports teams had a wide pool of talent, with very few individuals remaining at an amateur level. As a result, it was relatively easy to identify talent and help it emerge at a competitive or professional level. Today, however, the situation is quite the opposite.

Within groups, we see far fewer standout talents. Selection is becoming less a matter of choosing among many promising athletes and more a necessity for maintaining competitive and professional standards, as most students and athletes remain at an amateur level.

What are the causes?

  • Excessive screen time: too little time spent playing outdoors or in the streets, where children can develop a broad range of physical and social skills.
  • Poor dietary habits: high consumption of junk food, contributing to increased obesity and related health issues.
  • Lack of willingness to embrace hard work: an increasing reluctance to accept the demanding work and sacrifices essential to developing both the athlete and the individual as a whole.
