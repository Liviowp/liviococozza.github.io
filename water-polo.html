Professional Profile

From Fundamental Skills to Elite Performance

With over 20 years of international experience in swimming and water polo, I have worked across a wide spectrum of aquatic sports, from beginner-level programmes such as AquaGoal and foundational skill development to competitive, professional and national-team athletes.

My experience combines coaching, technical leadership, athlete development, performance training, programme management and staff supervision across different countries and sporting environments.

I believe in developing athletes through a progressive approach combining technical excellence, tactical understanding, physical preparation, game intelligence and a strong foundation of fundamental skills.

Coaching Methodology

Building the Complete Player

Developing fundamental skills, connecting them to game situations and helping athletes understand the decisions behind the action.

01

Technical Skills

Swimming and movement, body position, ball handling, passing, shooting, individual skills and goalkeeper development.

02

Tactical Learning

Positioning, space awareness, game principles, offensive and defensive organisation, decision-making and tactical application.

03

Physical Preparation

Swimming conditioning, strength, mobility, coordination, athletic preparation and performance-focused training.

04

Team & Programme Development

Annual planning, macrocycles, staff collaboration, mentoring, club strategy and sustainable programme growth.
