ABOUT ME

A Coach. An Educator. A Programme Builder.

I am an international sports professional with over 20 years of experience in swimming, water polo and physical education. My career has taken me across Italy, the United Kingdom, the United States, Germany and Belgium, allowing me to develop a broad international perspective on coaching, athlete development, sports education and programme management.

With a Bachelor's Degree in Sports and Exercise Sciences from the University of Perugia, and coaching qualifications including ASCA, ASA, DSV and FIN, I combine academic knowledge with extensive practical experience in competitive sport and education.

Throughout my career, I have held positions as Head Coach, Technical Director, Swimming Coach and Physical Education Teacher, working with athletes of different ages and performance levels, from grassroots development to high-performance environments.

My professional journey includes supporting the Italian National Team during the Mediterranean Games, coaching in the British National League, leading a women's water polo team in Germany, developing competitive swimming programmes in the United States and Italy, and leading an U18 team to a National Championship Gold Medal in Belgium. I have also completed coaching placements with the Spain U19 Men's National Water Polo Team, Ferencvárosi Water Polo Club in Hungary, and Posillipo Water Polo Club, Italy.

Beyond coaching, I am passionate about developing people, building successful programmes and sharing knowledge. Through WPSMethodology, my technical publications and international educational activities, I aim to contribute to the continuous development of swimming and water polo.

I believe that successful coaching goes beyond performance and results. It is about creating opportunities, developing potential, building strong teams and inspiring people to achieve more than they thought possible.
