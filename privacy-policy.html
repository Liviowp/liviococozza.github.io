Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This notice explains how personal information is handled when you visit liviococozza.com or contact Livio Cocozza through the website.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The data controller is Livio Cocozza, acting as a natural person in connection with this personal professional portfolio.

Contact: doriamondo@gmail.com. A residential address is not published on this website. You may use the email address above for privacy requests.

2. What information may be collected?

  • Contact form: name, email address, the type of organisation you represent, the objective selected and the message you submit.
  • Email correspondence: the information you choose to include in an email.
  • Technical information: information that may be processed by the website hosting provider or network infrastructure to deliver, secure and troubleshoot the website, such as IP address, browser/device information and server logs.
  • External resources: the website's font files are self-hosted and are not requested from Google Fonts.

Please do not submit sensitive personal information or information about children through the contact form.

3. Why is information used and what is the legal basis?

  • To read and respond to enquiries about employment, coaching, consultancy or professional opportunities. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a possible contract, or legitimate interests in managing professional correspondence, as appropriate.
  • To operate, maintain and protect the website. The legal basis is legitimate interests in providing a secure and functioning website.
  • To comply with legal obligations, where applicable.

Your information is not used by this website for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Contact form and service providers

The contact form is submitted through Web3Forms. Information entered into the form — name, email address, organisation/representative type, selected objective and message — is transmitted to Web3Forms so the enquiry can be delivered to the site owner. The notification email received by the site owner may also include the visitor's IP address, as shown in the form notification generated during testing. The visitor is not asked to enter this IP address; it is technical information included in the notification by the form service. For enquiries submitted through the form, Livio Cocozza acts as the data controller. Web3Creative, the operator of Web3Forms, states that it acts as a data processor. Web3Forms identifies Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare and Hetzner as core infrastructure sub-processors. Its privacy notice states that submitters’ IP addresses and email addresses may be shared with CleanTalk and Akismet for spam prevention. Optional integrations such as Google Sheets, Telegram, Discord or Slack may receive form data if enabled in the Web3Forms account; the Web3Forms account settings reviewed on 24 November 2026 show that no optional integration is enabled. The available integration options reviewed included Google Sheets, Telegram, Slack, Discord and Webhooks. The website owner's Web3Forms account is configured to permanently delete submissions older than three months. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy and its Data Processing Agreement.

The website is maintained in a GitHub repository and may be delivered through GitHub Pages or associated hosting/network infrastructure. Those providers may process technical request data for hosting, security and delivery. See GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website's fonts are hosted locally on this website and are not requested from Google Fonts. The website's flag images are now hosted locally on the website and are not requested from FlagCDN. See Google Privacy Policy.

The website also links to external services such as LinkedIn, Facebook and YouTube. Following those links takes you to the relevant provider's website, which processes data under its own terms. No embedded social-media feed is intentionally used in the homepage code reviewed for this notice.

5. International transfers

Web3Forms states that its operator, Web3Creative, is based in India and that personal data from the European Economic Area may be processed or transferred outside the EEA through its own operations or infrastructure providers. Its Data Processing Agreement incorporates the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers where required. The DPA lists AWS, Cloudflare, Hetzner, CleanTalk and Akismet among its sub-processors, with locations and roles described in that document. This is the provider's published transfer framework; the site owner has not independently audited the actual processing locations, account configuration or transfer risk assessment. Optional integrations may involve additional providers if enabled. See the Web3Forms Data Processing Agreement.

6. How long is information kept?

Enquiry information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage the professional conversation and document any resulting engagement. Information may be retained longer where required by law or where necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. Technical logs are retained according to the hosting and infrastructure providers' retention practices.

The website owner's Web3Forms account is currently configured to permanently delete form submissions older than three months. This setting is intended to limit the retention of enquiries in the Web3Forms account. Copies of correspondence that the site owner separately keeps in an email inbox or other records are not automatically deleted by this Web3Forms setting and are retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above. Web3Forms and its infrastructure providers may retain technical or security logs under their own policies.

7. Your data protection rights

Subject to the conditions and limitations in applicable law, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time; withdrawal does not affect processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise your rights, email doriamondo@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection supervisory authority. If the Belgian authority is competent, its website is dataprotectionauthority.be.

8. Security

Reasonable measures are used to maintain the website and protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the website, its service providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.
